### Ready-to-Use Prompts

-   **If you have messy config text or code:**
    
    > _"Extract just the direct audio stream URL from this line so I can paste it into a radio player: `[paste your snippet here]`"_
    
-   **If a stream URL fails to play in your radio:**
    
    > _"This radio stream URL isn't playing in my player: `[https://drive.uber.radio/uber/calmclassical/icecast.audio](https://drive.uber.radio/uber/calmclassical/icecast.audio)`. Can you provide the direct HTTP/HTTPS stream link, playlist URL (.pls or .m3u), or working alternatives for this station?"_
    
-   **If you know the station name but not the stream link:**
    
    > _"What is the direct MP3 or AAC Icecast stream URL for [Station Name, e.g., 'Uber Radio Calm Classical'] that works in standalone hardware or internet radio apps?"_

## Example 1

* Explain the difference between a library and a bookstore like I am five years old.
* I want to start a small vegetable garden in my backyard. I live in a sunny area with sandy soil. Can you tell me five easy vegetables to plant, how often to water them, and what tools I need to buy from the store?
* Write a short, funny caption for a photo of a sleepy cat.
* Tell me a joke.

## Example 2

* Comparison: Use this style when you want to understand how two things are different or which one is better for a specific need. Example: Compare a car to a bicycle.
* A lot of detail: Use this when you need a very specific result or a plan that fits your exact situation. Example: Write a workout plan for a 40-year-old beginner who has a knee injury and only has dumbbells at home.
* One short line: Use this for quick tasks, simple edits, or fast summaries. Example: Rewrite this email to sound more professional.
* Just a few words: Use this for basic facts, brainstorming, or quick look-ups. Example: Capital of France?

## Example 3

| Type | Basic Prompt | Better Prompt | Why it is Better |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Comparison | Compare cats and dogs. | Compare the monthly cost and daily time commitment required to care for a cat versus a dog. | It defines specific attributes, which prevents the AI from giving a generic answer. |
| Long Prompt | Write a story about space. | Write a 300-word story about a lonely astronaut on Mars who finds a mysterious plant, focusing on the feeling of isolation. | It provides a length constraint, a specific plot point, and a desired emotional tone. |
| Short Prompt | Give me some health tips. | Give me three actionable tips for improving my sleep hygiene that I can start tonight. | It quantifies the output and makes the request time-bound and practical. |
| Very Short Prompt | Fix this. | Proofread this. | Using a precise action verb removes ambiguity about whether you want the text shortened, corrected, or rewritten. |

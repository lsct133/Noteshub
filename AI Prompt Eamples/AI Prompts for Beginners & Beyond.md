## Example 1: Four Easy AI Prompt Styles for Beginners

### Comparison Prompt
**"Compare solar energy and wind energy in terms of cost, efficiency, and environmental impact. What are the pros and cons of each?"**

This style works when you want to understand differences and make decisions between options.

### Long, Detailed Prompt
**"I'm planning a vegetable garden for the first time. I live in a temperate climate with moderate sunlight (about 6 hours per day). I have a small backyard about 4 by 6 meters. I want to grow tomatoes, lettuce, and herbs. Please explain what soil I need, how much space each plant requires, watering schedules, and common mistakes beginners make."**

This style works when your situation is specific and you need detailed, personalized guidance.

### Short-Line Prompt
**"What are the best practices for remote team communication?"**

This style works when you have a clear question but don't need extensive context.

### Very Short Prompt
**"Explain photosynthesis."**

This style works for straightforward topics where you just need a basic explanation.

## Example 2: A Simple Guide for New AI Users

### Understanding Different Prompt Styles

| Prompt Style | When to Use It | Example | Why It Works |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Comparison** | You need to choose between options or understand differences | "Compare Python vs. JavaScript for web development" | AI can weigh multiple factors and help you decide |
| **Long & Detailed** | Your situation is specific and you need tailored help | "I'm a freelancer with 5 years of design experience looking to transition to UX. I have basic coding knowledge but no formal training. What's a realistic 6-month learning plan?" | Context helps AI give personalized, relevant advice instead of generic answers |
| **One Short Line** | You have a clear question but minimal context needed | "How do I format a citation in APA style?" | Efficient and clear—AI understands what you need without extra details |
| **Just a Few Words** | You want quick facts or definitions | "Capital of Australia" | Perfect for simple lookups or when you're testing the AI |

### The Key Principle
**More context = more helpful answers.** The AI works best when it understands your goal, your situation, and what you actually need.

## Example 3: Expert Tips — Basic vs. Better Prompts

An expert knows that **how you ask determines what you get.** Here's how to level up your prompts:

### Comparison Prompts

**❌ Basic:**
"Compare tablets and laptops"

**✅ Better:**
"I'm a student who takes notes in class and writes research papers. I have a budget of $600. Compare tablets and laptops in terms of note-taking experience, typing comfort, portability, and battery life. Which would be better for my use case?"

**Why the better one works:** It adds context (student, specific tasks), budget, and criteria. The AI now knows *why* you're comparing and can give advice tailored to your actual needs, not just generic differences.

---

### Long, Detailed Prompts

**❌ Basic:**
"Tell me about starting a business. Include everything."

**✅ Better:**
"I want to start a freelance copywriting business. I have 3 years of marketing experience, about $2,000 to invest, and I can work evenings/weekends while keeping my current job. Walk me through: registering the business, setting up a portfolio, finding first clients, pricing my services, and managing taxes. What are the biggest mistakes freelancers make in year one?"

**Why the better one works:** It's specific about the business type, your background, your constraints, and what you actually need. The AI delivers a focused roadmap instead of vague general advice about "starting a business."

---

### Short-Line Prompts

**❌ Basic:**
"How do I exercise?"

**✅ Better:**
"What's an effective 30-minute home workout for building strength with no equipment?"

**Why the better one works:** It adds just enough detail (duration, location, goal, constraints) to eliminate guesswork. The AI knows exactly what you need.

---

### Very Short Prompts

**❌ Basic:**
"Python"

**✅ Better:**
"What is Python used for?"

**Why the better one works:** Even very short prompts benefit from being *complete questions*. A single word forces the AI to guess; a simple question is always clearer.

## The Expert's Golden Rules

1. **Always include your goal.** Don't just ask what something is—say why you need to know.

2. **Add constraints and context.** Budget, skill level, timeline, location, and limitations help AI give relevant answers.

3. **Be specific about what you want.** "Compare X and Y for my situation" beats "Compare X and Y."

4. **Ask follow-up questions if the first answer isn't quite right.** AI improves when you give feedback.

5. **Match detail to complexity.** Simple questions need simple prompts; complex situations need rich context.

**The pattern:** Better prompts aren't necessarily longer—they're *smarter*. They show the AI what you actually care about.

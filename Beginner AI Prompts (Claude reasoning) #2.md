### Beginner AI Prompts (Claude reasoning) #2

* Comparison: Compare the health benefits of drinking green tea versus black tea.
* Long: I am planning a three-day weekend trip to New Orleans in March. I enjoy jazz music, Creole architecture, and spicy food, but I prefer to avoid the most crowded tourist traps. Can you suggest a relaxed itinerary with a few hidden gems?
* Short line: Write a four-line poem about a rainy afternoon.
* Very short: Tell me a joke.

## Guide for New AI Users
| Style | Example | When to Use |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Comparison | Compare a hybrid car to an electric car. | When you are trying to make a decision between two options or understand the differences between two concepts. |
| Detailed | Act as a career coach. Review my experience in marketing and suggest three new industries where my skills would be valuable, explaining why for each. | When you need a high-quality, specific result that requires context, a certain persona, or a particular format. |
| Short Line | Summarize the main plot of the book 1984. | When you have a straightforward request and the AI already has the necessary information to answer without extra context. |
| Few Words | Define quantum entanglement. | When you need a quick fact, a definition, or a simple trigger to start a broader conversation. |

## Expert Prompting Tips
| Prompt Type | Basic Prompt | Better Prompt | Why it is Better |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Comparison | Compare dogs and cats. | Compare dogs and cats as pets for someone living in a small city apartment, focusing on noise levels and exercise needs. | It adds a specific scenario (city apartment) and specific criteria (noise/exercise), leading to a practical answer rather than a generic list. |
| Long Prompt | Write a workout plan. | Create a 4-week strength training program for a beginner with no gym equipment. Include three sessions per week and focus on core stability and upper body strength. | It provides constraints (no equipment), a timeframe (4 weeks), and a specific goal (core and upper body), which prevents the AI from suggesting unattainable exercises. |
| Short Prompt | Write a story. | Write a 200-word mystery story that begins with a character finding a locked box in their attic. | It sets a length limit and a specific plot starting point, giving the AI a creative direction to follow. |
| Very Short Prompt | Explain AI. | Explain AI to a five-year-old. | It specifies the target audience, which forces the AI to simplify the language and use relatable analogies. |

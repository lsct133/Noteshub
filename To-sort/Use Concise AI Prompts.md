Use concise A.I prompts

  > Using concise prompts avoids wasting free quotas. It also saves you from
> typing long questions, when a short one would have been sufficient

ANALOGY

"I suppose it's like finding a shortcut to the local shops instead of always
walking the long way around. But how do you find that shortcut? There are no
Google Maps for AI prompts, and there are no neighbours to living next to you in
your AI chat box."

EXAMPLE OF A LONG PROMPT:

I'm confused, help me with a maths question. If a product costs me £30.00 per
month for 12 months. i use the following method to work out how much it would
cost for a whole year. My work out method is this: 3 x 12 = 36 then add a zero
so it costs £360 for a whole year - Quicker than 12 x 30 or 30 x 12. However, If
it was £300 per month for 12 months I cant figure that out. Help appreciate

A CONCISE PROMPT TO USE (ONE LINE):

"Help: If something costs £30/month for 12 months I calculate 3×12=36 then add a
zero → £360. Explain why that works, and show how to use the same quick method
for £300/month for 12 months."

OPTIONAL EVEN SHORTER:

"Explain why 3×12 then add a zero gives £360 for £30/month×12, and apply the
same trick to £300/month×12."

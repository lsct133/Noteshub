> 10,772 people = 12.3% of the total population.

To find the total population, you reverse the percentage calculation:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Step-by-step:
 1. Write the equation:10,772=12.3%×Total&nbsp;Population 10,772 = 12.3\% \times
    \text{Total Population}

    Population} = \frac{10,772}{0.123}

Alright — here’s the mental math shortcut for problems like:
“X people is Y% of the total — find total.”

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------


SHORTCUT METHOD

If Part=Percent×Total\text{Part} = \text{Percent} \times \text{Total}
then
Total=Part×100Percent\text{Total} = \frac{\text{Part} \times
100}{\text{Percent}}

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

For your example:
 1. Multiply 10,772 × 100→ 1,077,200
 2. Divide by 12.3
    * 12 × 7,300 ≈ 87,600 (close enough!)
    * More precisely: 1,077,200 ÷ 12.3 ≈ 87,562

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

💡 Trick to speed it up
 * Round the percent to something friendly (e.g., 12 instead of 12.3) to
   estimate.
 * Then adjust:
   * If your rounded percent was smaller than the actual, your estimate will be
     too big — so shrink it slightly.
   * If your rounded percent was bigger, bump your estimate up a bit.

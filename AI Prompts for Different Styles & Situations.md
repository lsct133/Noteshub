## 1. Easy AI Prompts for Beginners

Here are four beginner-friendly prompts in different styles:

### Comparison Prompt
"Compare solar energy and wind energy in terms of cost, efficiency, and environmental impact. What are the main advantages and disadvantages of each?"

### Long, Detailed Prompt
"I'm planning a vegetable garden in a small urban space and want to grow tomatoes, peppers, and herbs. I have about 4 hours of direct sunlight per day, limited water access, and I'm a complete beginner. Please provide a step-by-step guide for setting up container gardening, including what soil to use, how often to water, when to plant, and what containers work best for each type of plant."

### Short One-Line Prompt
"How do I improve my public speaking skills?"

### Very Short Prompt
"Best coffee brewing methods?"

## 2. Simple Guide for New AI Users

| **Style** | **When to Use It** | **Example** |
|-----------|-------------------|-----------|
| **Comparison** | When you want to understand the differences or similarities between two or more things. Best for evaluating options or learning contrasts. | "Compare Python and JavaScript for web development." |
| **Long & Detailed** | When you have a complex situation with specific context, constraints, or multiple requirements. Gives the AI enough information to provide a tailored, thorough answer. | "I'm a student with ADHD, limited budget, and 2 hours to study for a biology exam on cell division. What study techniques would work best for my learning style?" |
| **Short One-Line** | When you want a straightforward answer to a clear question. Efficient for general knowledge or simple how-tos. | "What's the capital of Australia?" |
| **Very Short** | When you want quick, casual information or reminders. Fast for simple lookups or brainstorming. | "Best productivity apps?" |

## 3. Expert Perspective: Basic vs. Better Prompts

### **Type 1: Comparison Prompts**

| **Basic Prompt** | **Better Prompt** | **Why It's Better** |
|-----------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| "Compare cats and dogs." | "Compare cats and dogs as apartment pets. Consider noise level, exercise needs, maintenance time, and cost over 5 years. Which would be better for a busy professional?" | The better prompt specifies context (apartment living, busy professional), adds specific criteria (noise, exercise, time, cost), and clarifies the purpose (which is better for this person). This gives the AI a clear frame to work within instead of a generic comparison. |

### **Type 2: Long, Detailed Prompts**

| **Basic Prompt** | **Better Prompt** | **Why It's Better** |
|-----------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| "How do I start a business?" | "I want to start a freelance graphic design business with $500 startup budget, working part-time while employed. I have 3 years of design experience, Adobe skills, but no clients yet. I have 10 hours per week available. What's a realistic first-year roadmap including pricing strategy, finding initial clients, tools I'll need, and a timeline?" | The better prompt eliminates ambiguity by providing specifics: budget, timeline availability, current skills, goals, and what you actually need help with. The AI can give a realistic, actionable plan instead of generic startup advice. |

### **Type 3: Short One-Line Prompts**

| **Basic Prompt** | **Better Prompt** | **Why It's Better** |
|-----------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| "How do I lose weight?" | "What are evidence-based ways to lose weight sustainably for someone with a sedentary office job?" | The better prompt adds one key detail (sedentary job) that completely changes the relevance of the answer. Instead of generic advice, you get tips suited to your actual situation. |

### **Type 4: Very Short Prompts**

| **Basic Prompt** | **Better Prompt** | **Why It's Better** |
|-----------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| "Dinner ideas?" | "Dinner ideas using chicken, rice, and vegetables—30 minutes, family of 4?" | Adding just a few details (what you have, how much time, how many people) transforms a vague request into something the AI can answer practically and specifically. The AI knows exactly what constraints to work with. |

---

## Key Takeaway

**The better prompts all follow one rule: give the AI enough context to give you the right answer.** Even adding one or two details—a constraint, a time frame, your situation, or what you'll do with the answer—dramatically improves the response. Think of it as helping the AI help you.

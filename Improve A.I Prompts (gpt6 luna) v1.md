You don’t need special skills to use AI—clear, simple instructions work well. Here are four beginner-friendly prompt styles, with a basic version and a stronger one for each.

| Style | Basic prompt | Better prompt | Why it helps |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Use a comparison** | “Explain saving money.” | “Explain the difference between saving and investing using a simple comparison, like a piggy bank and a garden. Keep it beginner-friendly.” | The comparison gives AI a helpful way to make an unfamiliar idea easier to picture. |
| **Give lots of detail** | “Help me save money.” | “Help me make a simple monthly savings plan. I earn about $2,000 a month, pay $1,500 in regular bills, and want to save for a car. Suggest realistic steps and use plain language.” | Relevant details help AI tailor its answer. Leave out anything you don’t want to share. |
| **Use one short line** | “Photosynthesis?” | “Explain photosynthesis in one simple sentence.” | A short prompt can work well when you also say what kind of answer you want. |
| **Use just a few words** | “Dinner ideas.” | “Easy vegetarian dinners, 20 minutes.” | A few specific words can be enough for a quick list. |

A useful pattern is: **what you want + any helpful details + how you want the answer presented**. For example: “Suggest three easy dinners using rice and beans. Keep each recipe under 20 minutes.”
